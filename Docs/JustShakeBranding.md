Prompt:

> Edit this exact cola bottle desktop icon. Retain its single-bottle silhouette, red ribbed cap, very dark green cola bottle, red label with plain cream stripe, orientation and approximate framing. Replace the glossy rendered treatment with restrained, flat, handmade retro game pixel art: matte local colors, simple deliberate pixel clusters, only 2 or 3 closely related value steps per material to define form. Absolutely remove yellow/orange rim lighting, reflections, specular highlights, shiny spots, bloom, glow, cast shadows, directional lighting and metallic sheen. Remove scratches, distressed label flecks, painterly noise and busy texture. Keep a clean readable low-resolution look, no lettering or logo, no border and no other objects. Minimal and ordinary, like an artist-authored sprite rather than a cinematic 3D illustration. Genuine transparent background, square canvas.
