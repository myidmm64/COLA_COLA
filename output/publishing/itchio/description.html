ONE BOTTLE. TWO SIDES. JUST SHAKE.

A cola bottle sits between you and your opponent in a dim pizzeria. Shake harder for a bigger reward, or play it safe and leave the pressure for the other side. If it bursts on your turn, you take the damage.

JUST SHAKE. is a single-player, turn-based game of risk and resource management with a PS1-style atmosphere.

Read the pressure. Change the odds.

  • Three shake strengths. Choose your risk, watch the pressure gauge, and decide when to push your luck.
  • Items that change the table. Cool the cola, raise your opponent’s risk, pass a turn, or make the next burst hurt more.
  • Ring for supplies. Spend tokens at the waiter bell to receive random items. Your opponent can order and use items too.
  • Build your run. Choose an ability after every three bursts across both sides, then carry your remaining items and tokens into the next match.
  • Clear five stages. Face a growing challenge and discover 26 achievements along the way.

Controls

  • 1 / 2 / 3: choose shake strength, or click the buttons.
  • Space / click the bottle: shake the cola.
  • Hover: inspect an object.
  • Left-click: use an item or ring the waiter bell.
  • Right-click your item: return it for 1 token.
  • Tab: abilities and achievements (or use the button in the web version).
  • Esc: settings (or click Settings in the web version).

Play in your browser or download for Windows

For Windows, extract the entire ZIP and open JUST SHAKE.exe. Keep the executable and its accompanying files together.

Languages: English · 한국어 · 日本語 · 简体中文 · 繁體中文 · Français · Deutsch · Español · Português (Brasil) · Русский. Change your language in Settings.

Content note: sudden loud cola bursts, screen flashes, and camera shake.
